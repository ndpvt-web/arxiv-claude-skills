## Core Capability

## Workflow

1. Parse the user's information need or query
2. Formulate effective search strategies
3. Retrieve relevant documents, code, or data
4. Synthesize findings into a coherent response
5. Provide citations and references

## Research Context

> Due to recent advancements in Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) that demonstrate remarkable performance across a range of sound-, speech- and music-related tasks, there is a growing interest in proposing benchmarks to assess these models. Existing benchmarks generally focus only on reasoning with internal knowledge, neglecting real-world scenarios that require external information grounding. To bridge this gap, we introduce AudioRAG, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate audio-based reasoning
