## Research Context

> Sentence-level human value detection is typically framed as multi-label classification over Schwartz values, but it remains unclear whether Schwartz higher-order (HO) categories provide usable structure. We study this under a strict compute-frugal budget (single 8 GB GPU) on ValueEval'24 / ValuesML (74K English sentences). We compare (i) direct supervised transformers, (ii) HO$\rightarrow$values pipelines that enforce the hierarchy with hard masks, and (iii) Presence$\rightarrow$HO$\rightarrow$v
