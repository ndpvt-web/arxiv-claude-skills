# Agent-Supported Foresight for AI Systemic Risks: AI Agents for Breadth, Experts for Judgment

**Source:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08565v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08565v1)
**Category:** cs.HC | **Published:** 2026-02-09 | **Skill Score:** 58
**Authors:** Leon Fröhling, Alessandro Giaconia, Edyta Paulina Bogucka...

## Core Capability

Search, retrieve, and synthesize information.

## Key Techniques

- **Proposed technique:** a scalable approach that simulates in-silico agents using the strategic foresight method of the futures wheel

## Workflow

1. Parse the user's information need or query
2. Formulate effective search strategies
3. Retrieve relevant documents, code, or data
4. Synthesize findings into a coherent response
5. Provide citations and references

## Research Context

> AI impact assessments often stress near-term risks because human judgment degrades over longer horizons, exemplifying the Collingridge dilemma: foresight is most needed when knowledge is scarcest. To address long-term systemic risks, we introduce a scalable approach that simulates in-silico agents using the strategic foresight method of the Futures Wheel. We applied it to four AI uses spanning Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs): Chatbot Companion (TRL 9, mature), AI Toy (TRL 7, medium), Griefbot

Refer to the [full paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08565v1) for detailed methodology.
