# CLM-Bench: Benchmarking and Analyzing Cross-lingual Misalignment of LLMs in Knowledge Editing

**Source:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17397v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17397v1)
**Category:** cs.CL | **Published:** 2026-01-24 | **Skill Score:** 60
**Authors:** Yucheng Hu, Wei Zhou, Juesi Xiao

## Core Capability

Search, retrieve, and synthesize information.

## Workflow

1. Parse the user's information need or query
2. Formulate effective search strategies
3. Retrieve relevant documents, code, or data
4. Synthesize findings into a coherent response
5. Provide citations and references

## Research Context

> Knowledge Editing (KE) has emerged as a promising paradigm for updating facts in Large Language Models (LLMs) without retraining. However, progress in Multilingual Knowledge Editing (MKE) is currently hindered by biased evaluation frameworks. We observe that existing MKE benchmarks are typically constructed by mechanically translating English-centric datasets into target languages (e.g., English-to-Chinese). This approach introduces translation artifacts and neglects culturally specific entities
