# Dancing in Chains: Strategic Persuasion in Academic Rebuttal via Theory of Mind

**Source:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15715v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15715v2)
**Category:** cs.CL | **Published:** 2026-01-22 | **Skill Score:** 69
**Authors:** Zhitao He, Zongwei Lyu, Yi R Fung

## Core Capability

Search, retrieve, and synthesize information.

## Workflow

1. Parse the user's information need or query
2. Formulate effective search strategies
3. Retrieve relevant documents, code, or data
4. Synthesize findings into a coherent response
5. Provide citations and references

## Agent Coordination

- Decompose complex tasks into independent subtasks where possible
- Use parallel execution for independent subtasks
- Implement error recovery and retry logic
- Aggregate partial results when full completion fails

## Research Context

> Although artificial intelligence (AI) has become deeply integrated into various stages of the research workflow and achieved remarkable advancements, academic rebuttal remains a significant and underexplored challenge. This is because rebuttal is a complex process of strategic communication under severe information asymmetry rather than a simple technical debate. Consequently, current approaches struggle as they largely imitate surface-level linguistics, missing the essential element of perspect
